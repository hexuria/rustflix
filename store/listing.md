## Description

rustflix is a beautiful, fast way to discover and watch movies, TV shows and
anime — all from one app.

DISCOVER
- Trending, popular and top-rated movies, shows and anime
- Search across everything, or browse by genre
- Rich detail pages with posters, ratings, cast and trailers

WATCH
- Multiple streaming sources per title — pick the one that works for you
- Movies, full TV seasons and episodes, and anime with sub/dub support
- Built-in player with gesture controls: double-tap to seek, swipe-friendly
  timeline, playback speed, subtitles and fullscreen
- Continue watching picks up exactly where you left off

ORGANIZE
- Build your watchlist and library
- Downloads for offline viewing
- Dark, native iOS design

rustflix is a metadata browser and player — it does not host any content.
Streaming sources are provided by third-party services.
